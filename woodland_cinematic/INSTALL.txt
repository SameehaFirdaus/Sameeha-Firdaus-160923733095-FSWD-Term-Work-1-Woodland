WOODLAND CINEMATIC HERO - QUICK INSTALL

1. Copy commercial-hero.css into your project's css folder.
2. Copy hero-commercial.js into your project's js folder.
3. In index.html, add this AFTER animation.css/responsive.css:
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="css/commercial-hero.css">
4. Replace ONLY the existing <section class="hero"> ... </section> with the contents of hero-section.html.
5. At the bottom of index.html, keep cart.js and main.js, and add:
   <script src="js/hero-commercial.js"></script>
6. Do NOT change products.html, cart.html, payment.html, cart.js, products.js, cart-page.js or payment.js.

Required existing assets:
assets/images/woodpecker-test.jpg
assets/images/hippo-stampede.jpg
